## Full description
Jeep Trails turns your wrist into a new off-road vista every day.

Seven PixelLab trail scenes rotate with the calendar: desert canyons, forest paths, mountain passes, beach dunes, snowy alpine roads, farm country, and Moab slickrock. The sky tracks the real clock—morning light, daytime blue, evening glow, and night with a moon and stars—while the time and date sit up in the sky.

Once each hour, a Jeep drives across the screen, bouncing along a terrain path sampled from that day’s trail. Open Clay settings to pick colors, show or hide the date, lock a favorite scene, choose which minute the drive starts, and optionally vibe when the Jeep rolls out.

Built for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2.
